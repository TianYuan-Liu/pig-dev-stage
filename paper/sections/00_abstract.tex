% Abstract
\begin{abstract}
We present a calibrated transcriptomic classifier for inferring porcine developmental stages across eight tissues.
By integrating PigGTEx RNA-seq profiles with machine learning, our framework achieves balanced accuracies of 0.82--0.96
and macro F1 scores of 0.76--0.95 across tissue-specific models. The classifier identifies tissue-resolved molecular
signatures that align with known developmental programs: adipogenesis markers in adipose, neurogenesis pathways in brain,
and myogenesis regulators in muscle. Validation with canonical biomarkers demonstrates qualitative concordance with human
developmental patterns based on literature, supporting the pig as a translational model. This molecular staging system provides an objective, scalable alternative to
morphological assessment for biomedical research and agricultural applications.
\end{abstract}
