GO enrichment of the stage-informative gene sets underscored strong tissue-specific developmental programs rather than a shared pan-tissue signature. Adipose predictors were dominated by regulators of adipogenesis, with nineteen of the top fifty markers mapping to `fat cell differentiation` (FE ≈ 96, q = 5.8 × 10^-33) and complementary lipid-storage pathways (`triglyceride biosynthetic process`, FE ≈ 51), indicating that temporal progression in adipose is captured through maturation of lipid handling. Blood relied on hematopoietic circuitry: the most enriched terms included `hemopoiesis` (FE ≈ 86, q = 6.0 × 10^-25) and `erythrocyte differentiation` (FE ≈ 36), highlighting shifting immune and erythroid lineages across stages. Brain stage markers clustered around neurodevelopment, with high-magnitude enrichment for `synaptic transmission` (FE ≈ 69, q = 5.3 × 10^-25) and `neurogenesis` (FE ≈ 78), reflecting progressive neuronal wiring and synapse specification. Liver genes pointed to metabolic and hepatic differentiation programs, including `glycolytic process` (FE ≈ 44, q = 5.7 × 10^-19), `cholesterol homeostasis`, and `hepatocyte differentiation`, consistent with functional maturation of hepatocytes.

Respiratory tissue exhibited a distinct developmental axis: lung predictors were enriched for `lung development` (FE ≈ 34, q = 6.8 × 10^-24) and `respiratory system development`, while also capturing mitochondrial respiration (`cellular respiration`, FE ≈ 16), indicating coordination between structural remodeling and metabolic capacity. Muscle relied on myogenesis, including `skeletal muscle fiber development` (FE ≈ 24, q = 1.5 × 10^-19) and `muscle contraction`, emphasizing sarcomere assembly as the differentiating feature. Small intestine markers were skewed toward epithelial maturation and nutrient handling, such as `transmembrane transport` (FE ≈ 25, q = 3.6 × 10^-20), `intestinal epithelial cell differentiation`, and `digestion`. Testis predictors were uniquely enriched for reproductive pathways—`spermatid development` (FE ≈ 21, q = 5.6 × 10^-17) and `male gonad development`—alongside phosphorylation cascades, mirroring the timing of germ cell maturation. Together, these profiles show that the classifier learns distinct, tissue-appropriate gene programs to resolve developmental time, yet each program converges on processes that drive the maturation of its respective organ.
