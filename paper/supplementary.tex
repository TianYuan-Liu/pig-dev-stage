  \caption{\textbf{Cross-species sensitivity analysis.}
  \textbf{a}, Mean Pearson correlation between pig and human developmental fold changes across different p-value thresholds. Error bars show min--max range across different numbers of genes tested.
  \textbf{b}, Bootstrap distribution of correlation coefficient (1,000 iterations) for the optimal parameter set ($p < 0.10$, $n = 36$ genes). Dashed line indicates mean correlation; dotted lines show 95\% confidence interval.}
