  \caption{\textbf{Independent biological validation of stage boundaries using a curated panel of 25 literature-supported postnatal marker genes.}
  Stage-wise $\log_2(\text{TPM}+1)$ trajectories (line with shaded SE band) for canonical markers of the four transition windows: T1 (suckling-to-weaning $\sim$21\,d), T2 (weaning-to-growth $\sim$60\,d), T3 (pubertal switch $\sim$150\,d), and T4 (adult maturation $\sim$365\,d). The vertical dashed line in each panel marks the literature-predicted transition. The final panel shows the per-gene observed-vs-expected window agreement (bar height indicates whether the gene's largest adjacent-stage change occurred at the predicted window). Of 30 marker $\times$ tissue pairs with adequate per-stage coverage ($\geq 5$ samples per flanking stage), 20/30 (67\%) matched the predicted direction of change and 10/30 (33\%) had their largest single-step change at the predicted transition; T1 (suckling$\rightarrow$weaning) had the highest window-match rate (4/7 = 57\%). The porcine myosin heavy-chain switch (\textit{MYH3}, \textit{MYH8}, \textit{MYH2}) was directionally correct but transitioned earlier than the assumed weaning boundary, consistent with the first-month perinatal isoform exchange documented in pig skeletal muscle. Full gene-level results are in \texttt{review/analyses/results/marker\_gene\_transitions.csv}.}
  \label{fig:S6}
\end{figure}
