# Cardiff University MPhil Thesis Template and Requirements Report

This report outlines the Master of Philosophy (MPhil) thesis requirements and template information for Cardiff University, based on the **Academic Regulations 2025/2026** and official university policies.

## 1. MPhil Degree Overview
The MPhil at Cardiff University is a research-based degree that represents a "critical evaluation and analysis of a body of knowledge" [1]. While similar to a PhD in its research intensity, it is more tightly defined for completion within a shorter timeframe.

### Key Facts
| Feature | Full-Time | Part-Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 1 - 2 years | Up to 3 years |
| **Thesis Length** | Up to 50,000 words | Up to 50,000 words |
| **Assessment** | Written thesis and oral viva voce examination | Written thesis and oral viva voce examination |

## 2. Thesis Requirements and Criteria
According to the **Academic Regulations 2025/2026**, the MPhil thesis must provide evidence of:

> 1. "A systematic understanding of knowledge, and a critical awareness of current problems and/or new insights, much of which is at, or informed by, the forefront of the academic discipline, field of study, or area of professional practice" [1].
> 2. "A comprehensive understanding of techniques applicable to the research or advanced scholarship" [1].
> 3. "Originality in the application of knowledge, together with a practical understanding of how established techniques of research and enquiry are used to create and interpret knowledge in the discipline" [1].
> 4. "Conceptual understanding that enables the critical evaluation of current research and advanced scholarship in the discipline, and of techniques and methodologies" [1].

## 3. Thesis Template and Format
Cardiff University provides a standard **Word Thesis Template** (often referred to as the 2018 version) to ensure consistency in formatting.

### Required Structure
Based on the official template and submission guidelines, the thesis must include the following sections in order:
1.  **Title Page**: Including the university logo, thesis title, author name, school, and the statement: *"A thesis submitted in fulfilment of the requirements for the award of Master of Philosophy"* [2].
2.  **Declarations and Statements**: Formal confirmation of originality and word count.
3.  **Abstract**: A summary of the research (typically up to 300 words).
4.  **Acknowledgements**: Optional section to thank supervisors and contributors.
5.  **Table of Contents**: Detailed list of chapters and sections.
6.  **List of Tables/Figures**: If applicable.
7.  **Main Body**: Divided into chapters (e.g., Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Conclusion).
8.  **References/Bibliography**: Using the school's preferred citation style.
9.  **Appendices**: Supplementary material.

### Technical Specifications
- **File Format**: The thesis must be submitted as a **single PDF file** for examination [3].
- **Word Count**: Up to **50,000 words** (excluding references and appendices) [1].

## 4. Submission Process
1.  **Notice of Submission**: Students must submit a "Notice of Submission" form at least three months before their intended submission date.
2.  **Electronic Submission**: The final thesis is submitted electronically via the university's designated portal (e.g., SIMS or the Intranet).
3.  **Final Deposit**: After successful examination and any required corrections, the final version must be deposited in the **ORCA** (Online Research @ Cardiff) repository [4].
