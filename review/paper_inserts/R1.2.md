To test whether the directional concordance reported above for skeletal
muscle extends to other tissues, we re-applied the same pipeline (strict
one-to-one ortholog mapping verified against Ensembl
Compara\cite{cardoso-moreira_gene_2019}, $\log_2$ fold-change of Infant
vs Adult in both species, FDR$<$0.10 and $|\log_2\mathrm{FC}|>0.5$ in both
species, Pearson correlation with 1{,}000-bootstrap 95\% CI) to the
Cardoso-Moreira~\textit{et~al.}\cite{cardoso-moreira_gene_2019}
developmental atlas (ArrayExpress E-MTAB-6814; 8{,}277 validated
\texttt{ortholog\_one2one} pairs). Of the three tissues highlighted by
the reviewer, lung is not present in this human dataset (it covers
brain, cerebellum, heart, kidney, liver, ovary, testis), and we
therefore could not perform a pig$\leftrightarrow$human lung comparison
using this atlas; we discuss this explicitly below. Brain (human
$n=7$ Infant + $9$ Adult postnatal samples) and liver ($n=4+6$) are the
two reviewer-requested tissues that are present and have viable
Infant/Adult bins. Heart ($n=2$ Adult samples) and kidney ($n=0$ Adult
samples, the atlas truncates at the \textit{school} stage) lack
Adult-bin coverage and could not be assessed.

In brain, $n=302$ of the 8{,}277 one-to-one orthologs passed the strict
double-species filter, with Pearson $r=0.26$ (95\% bootstrap CI 0.16--0.37,
$p=3.5\times 10^{-6}$) and 81.5\% (246/302) of these genes changing in
the same direction in both species (Supplementary Fig.~S7a). In liver,
the small human-side $n$ leaves the strict double-FDR filter empty; under
our pre-specified pig-anchored alternative (pig FDR$<$0.10 and
$|\log_2\mathrm{FC}|>0.5$ in both species), $n=941$ genes were tested
with Pearson $r=0.25$ (95\% CI 0.17--0.32; Supplementary Fig.~S7b).
Overlap with the original 66-gene muscle set is small (one gene in
brain---\textit{MAOB}; 14 genes in liver, dominated by developmental
downregulation markers \textit{POSTN}, \textit{NRK}, \textit{ADAMTS2},
\textit{PTK7}). The full forest plot across muscle (previously
published $r=0.69$, CI 0.54--0.80), brain, and liver is shown in
Supplementary Fig.~S7d; per-gene tables are in Supplementary
Table~S8.

Within the postnatal-lifespan window covered by both atlases, these
results indicate that the directional pig$\leftrightarrow$human
concordance of net infant-to-adult fold-change is detectable in brain
and liver as well as in muscle, but the per-tissue correlation
magnitudes are markedly smaller than for muscle. Two effects contribute:
(i)~the per-tissue postnatal $n$ in Cardoso-Moreira (typically
$\le$~15 samples spanning Infant + Adult bins combined) reduces the
gene-level reliability of human log$_2$ fold-changes relative to the
Schaiter~\textit{et~al.}\cite{schaiter_molecular_2024} muscle dataset
($n=11$ with concentrated infant-vs-adult biopsies); (ii)~brain and
liver postnatal development engage broader transcriptomic remodelling
than the tight ECM/myogenic module that drives the muscle signature,
so the $|\log_2\mathrm{FC}|>0.5$ threshold pulls in more stage-
asynchronous genes. The 81.5\% directional concordance in brain
nonetheless supports a robust species-shared maturation programme.
Lung concordance remains untested until a human postnatal lung
developmental cohort becomes available; pig-side staging of lung
samples by our LightGBM classifier (mean balanced
accuracy~$=$~0.89) is independent of this limitation.
