**Results addition (~120 words):**

To make the tissue-specific feature space directly inspectable, we ranked
the genes in each ordinal model by aggregate LightGBM importance and
annotated the top 20 per tissue via mygene.info and UniProt REST
(Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:S7}; row-z-scored trajectories in
Supplementary Figure~\ref{fig:S5}). The non-muscle models surface biologically
coherent stage-defining genes that were not previously discussed: in brain,
the ketogenic enzyme \textit{HMGCS2} (infant peak) and the Hippo-pathway
kinase \textit{LATS1} (post-pubertal peak); in liver, the
proline/ornithine-biosynthetic enzyme \textit{ALDH18A1} together with
\textit{COL1A1} (both infant-peaking); in lung, the matricellular factor
\textit{CCN5} (post-pubertal, fold change 2.71) alongside the oncofetal
RNA-binder \textit{IGF2BP2} (infant peak, shared with muscle); and in blood,
the ribosomal subunit \textit{RPLP1} (infant peak, fold change 3.14) and the
chemokine receptor \textit{CCR6} (post-pubertal).

**Discussion addition (~30 words):**

The tissue-specific feature lists are dominated by genes with established
roles in lineage-appropriate processes -- ketogenesis and Hippo signalling in
brain, proline biosynthesis and ECM in liver, alveolar maturation in lung,
and ribosome biogenesis followed by adaptive-immunity programs in blood --
arguing that the classifiers are reading genuine developmental physiology
rather than batch artefacts.
