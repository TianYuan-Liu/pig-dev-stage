\paragraph{One-to-one ortholog definition.}
Pig (\textit{Sus scrofa}, Sscrofa11.1) and human (\textit{Homo sapiens},
GRCh38) Ensembl gene identifiers were resolved to HGNC-style symbols via
\texttt{mygene.info} (Python \texttt{mygene} client). Candidate one-to-one
ortholog pairs were defined as gene symbols appearing exactly once in
each species' annotation (``shared-unique-symbol'' filter; 15{,}200
candidates). Each candidate was then verified against Ensembl
Compara\cite{cardoso-moreira_gene_2019} via the public REST homology
endpoint \texttt{/homology/id/sus\_scrofa/\{gene\_id\}} with
\texttt{target\_species=human, type=orthologues}, retaining only pairs
whose reciprocal Compara classification was \texttt{ortholog\_one2one}
(the Compara confidence-1 equivalent). The resulting validated set
(8{,}277 pairs; cached at
\texttt{review/analyses/results/pig\_human\_one\_to\_one\_orthologs.csv})
is used for all cross-species comparisons. The full validation
script with cache fall-back is at
\texttt{review/analyses/cross\_species\_all\_tissues.py}.

\paragraph{Human Cardoso-Moreira dataset preprocessing.}
We used the per-sample RPKM matrix \texttt{Human.RPKM.txt} from
ArrayExpress accession E-MTAB-6814 (Cardoso-Moreira~\textit{et~al.}
2019)\cite{cardoso-moreira_gene_2019}. Sample columns follow the
naming convention \texttt{<Tissue>.<stage>.<replicate>}; we restricted
to brain and liver columns (the two reviewer-requested non-muscle
tissues that are present in this atlas with viable Infant- and
Adult-bin coverage---lung is not included in the Cardoso-Moreira
human RPKM matrix). Prenatal samples (any stage label ending in
\texttt{wpc}) were discarded. Cardoso-Moreira postnatal stage labels
were mapped to our five-stage porcine scheme as follows:
\textit{newborn/infant/toddler}~$\rightarrow$~Infant;
\textit{school/youngTeenager}~$\rightarrow$~Pre-pubertal;
\textit{teenager/oldTeenager}~$\rightarrow$~Post-pubertal;
\textit{youngAdult/youngMidAge}~$\rightarrow$~Adult; and
\textit{olderMidAge/senior}~$\rightarrow$~Adult (oldest sub-bin).
Log$_2$ fold-changes (Adult/Infant) were computed in raw RPKM space
with a pseudocount of 0.01, matching the pig TPM pipeline. Per-gene
$p$-values were estimated by Welch's $t$-test on the human RPKM (small
$n$) and by Mann-Whitney $U$ on the pig TPM (large $n$); both were
Benjamini-Hochberg corrected within the orthologue universe. The pre-
specified strict filter (FDR$<$0.10 and $|\log_2\mathrm{FC}|>0.5$ in
both species) is identical to the muscle analysis; a pig-anchored
fallback (pig FDR$<$0.10 and $|\log_2\mathrm{FC}|>0.5$ in both
species, no human FDR) is reported for tissues where the small per-
tissue postnatal $n$ leaves human FDR underpowered (relevant for
liver).
