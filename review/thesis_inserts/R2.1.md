# Thesis insertion for R2.1 (Results / Discussion)

## For Results (chapter 4) - new subsection "Within-stage demographic heterogeneity does not invalidate marginal performance"

The chronological-age scheme used throughout this thesis groups samples into
five canonical bins (Infant, Early childhood, Pre-pubertal, Post-pubertal,
Adult). Because the underlying pigGTEx contributing studies were not
balanced for breed, sex or rearing context, each bin is internally
heterogeneous. To quantify this heterogeneity and test whether it biases
the per-tissue classifiers I performed a post-hoc stratification audit and
a subgroup-sensitivity replay of the published cross-validation predictions
(`review/analyses/heterogeneity_sensitivity.py`). The 5-fold outer split
and per-fold hyperparameters from the trained models were reused, so the
fold-level balanced accuracies are bit-identical to the values reported in
Section 4.X (Table 4.X).

Three findings emerge (Figure S8). First, the breed distribution shifts
substantively across stages: in muscle the dominant breed per stage moves
from Landrace (Infant, 89/194 = 46 %) to Duroc (Early childhood, 47/76 =
62 %) to Duroc x Pietrain (Post-pubertal, 167/396 = 42 %); in liver
similar shifts span 29-62 %. Second, the brain Infant set is 96 % a single
PIC cross (24/25), and several Blood / Lung stages are nearly monolithic
in declared breed. Third, the per-subgroup balanced accuracy varies
beyond what chance permutation predicts for muscle (Sex: observed
std(BA) = 0.096, p = 0.005; BioProject: 0.177, p = 0.014) and liver
(Breed: 0.152, p = 0.008), but is consistent with chance for brain/Sex,
liver/Sex and lung/Sex (all p >= 0.23). Per-subgroup BAs span 0.61
(muscle, Large white, n = 68) to 1.00 (muscle, Yorkshire, n = 12; liver,
Landrace, n = 51), with the marginal estimates remaining within the
overall ranges reported in Section 4.X.

## For Discussion (chapter 5) - amend the Limitations paragraph

I add the following sentences to the Limitations subsection: "The per-stage
classifier is calibrated to the breed and sex composition of pigGTEx, in
which no stage is a balanced mixture of breeds or sexes. A within-stage
permutation test demonstrated that muscle and liver subgroup BAs vary
beyond chance (muscle/Sex p = 0.005, muscle/BioProject p = 0.014, liver
/Breed p = 0.008). Deployment to cohorts dominated by under-represented
breeds (notably Large white for muscle, where BA = 0.61) or to studies
that aggregate pooled-sex tissue should therefore include re-calibration
on the new cohort. Future, prospectively balanced developmental atlases
across multiple commercial and indigenous breeds will be needed to give
a fully breed-agnostic statement."
