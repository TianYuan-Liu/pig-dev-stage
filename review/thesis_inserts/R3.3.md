\subsection*{Independent biological validation of stage boundaries}

The five developmental stages used throughout this thesis (Infant,
Early childhood, Pre-pubertal, Post-pubertal and Adult) are defined by
chronological age bins (0--20\,d, 21--59\,d, 60--149\,d, 150--365\,d and
>365\,d) intended to approximate well-recognised physiological milestones
in pig development. Classifier accuracy alone is not sufficient evidence
that these calendar boundaries coincide with the underlying biological
maturation switches: a classifier may simply pick up gradients in
expression that follow chronological age. To address this we conducted
an independent biological validation using a curated panel of 25
literature-supported postnatal mammalian marker genes whose expression is
expected to change sharply at one of the four transition windows in our
framework: suckling-to-weaning (\textit{T1}, $\sim$21\,d), weaning-to-growth
(\textit{T2}, $\sim$60\,d), pubertal switch (\textit{T3}, $\sim$150\,d) and
adult maturation (\textit{T4}, $\sim$365\,d).

For each marker $\times$ tissue pair, we extracted log$_2$(TPM+1) values
for the corresponding pig Ensembl gene from the PigGTEx expression
matrices (n = 761, 309, 78, 129 and 43 samples for muscle, liver, blood,
lung and brain respectively), computed mean and standard error per stage,
ran one-way ANOVA across stages and Tukey--HSD post-hoc pairwise
comparisons, and recorded the adjacent stage pair carrying the largest
mean difference as the observed maximum-change window. To prevent
small-stratum noise from driving conclusions, we excluded pairs whose
flanking stages each had fewer than 5 samples in that tissue. After this
filter, 30 marker $\times$ tissue pairs were evaluable.

Twenty of these thirty pairs (67\%) reproduced the literature-predicted
direction of change and ten (33\%) had their largest single-step change at
the exact predicted transition. The strongest concordance was observed at
the suckling-to-weaning boundary T1 (4/7 markers, 57\%), with very large
and statistically significant decreases of IGF2 (log$_2$\,FC $= -2.50$,
ANOVA $P < 10^{-130}$) and IGF1 in muscle and a parallel IGF2 reduction in
liver. The pubertal endocrine programme (T3) showed strong directional
agreement (7/8, 88\%), with AR, GHR, ESR1 and INSL3 all rising in their
expected tissues, although in muscle the steepest single-step rise often
occurred between Post-pubertal and the small Adult subset (T4) rather than
between Pre- and Post-pubertal. The most informative miss was the porcine
perinatal myosin-heavy-chain switch: MYH3 and MYH8 fell sharply and MYH2
rose sharply, in the directions predicted by the literature, but the
steepest change occurred between Infant (0--20\,d) and Early childhood
(21--59\,d) rather than between Early childhood and Pre-pubertal --
consistent with reports placing the perinatal isoform exchange within the
first postnatal month in pigs. No evaluable T2 marker had its largest
change at exactly day 60.

A full per-marker table is provided as
\texttt{review/analyses/results/marker\_gene\_transitions.csv} and a
four-panel trajectory figure as Supplementary Figure~S6. Together, these
observations supply partial independent biological support for the
five-stage framework -- especially the perinatal, peri-weaning and
pubertal switches -- while making clear that the chronological boundaries
are approximations of overlapping maturation programmes and that the
weaning boundary in particular may slightly post-date the steepest
porcine myosin switch.
