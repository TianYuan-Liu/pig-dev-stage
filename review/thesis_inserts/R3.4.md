**Results addition (~120 words):**

To make the tissue-specific feature space directly inspectable, the genes in
each calibrated ordinal model were ranked by aggregate LightGBM importance
and the top 20 per tissue were annotated via mygene.info and UniProt REST
(Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:S7}; row-z-scored trajectories in
Supplementary Figure~\ref{fig:S5}). The non-muscle models surface
biologically coherent stage-defining genes: in brain, the ketogenic enzyme
\textit{HMGCS2} (infant peak) and the Hippo-pathway kinase \textit{LATS1}
(post-pubertal peak); in liver, the proline/ornithine-biosynthetic enzyme
\textit{ALDH18A1} together with \textit{COL1A1} (both infant-peaking); in
lung, the matricellular factor \textit{CCN5} (post-pubertal, fold change
2.71) alongside the oncofetal RNA-binder \textit{IGF2BP2} (infant peak,
shared with muscle); and in blood, the ribosomal subunit \textit{RPLP1}
(infant peak, fold change 3.14) and the chemokine receptor \textit{CCR6}
(post-pubertal).

**Discussion addition (~30 words):**

These tissue-specific feature lists are dominated by genes with established
roles in lineage-appropriate processes -- ketogenesis and Hippo signalling in
brain, proline biosynthesis and ECM in liver, alveolar maturation in lung,
and ribosome biogenesis followed by adaptive-immunity programs in blood --
arguing that each classifier reads genuine developmental physiology rather
than batch artefacts and providing an immediately interpretable readout of
tissue-specific developmental molecular logic.
